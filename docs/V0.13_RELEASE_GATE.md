## Asset-licensing status

The software-side obligations are implemented and documented in
`THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md` and `docs/ASSET_LICENSE_AUDIT.md`.

The conservative residual is upstream provenance: the Z-Anatomy source does
not publish a per-object licence map, while its notice separately identifies a
small number of restricted contributed models. The committed export manifest
contains no names matching those kidney, inner-ear, brain/white-matter, or
cranial-nerve contributions. This is strong scope evidence but not a
per-vertex guarantee.

The MakeHuman/MPFB core body and system assets are covered by the project's
published CC0 statement. The selected skin is independently listed CC0. The
shorts are credited to Elvaerwyn under the MakeHuman catalogue's “CC-BY”
label; the catalogue omits a version.
