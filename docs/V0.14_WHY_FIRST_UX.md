# v0.14 — Why-first explorer UX

## Product intent

Eidome should not be a 3D model viewer. Every layer must answer:

1. What am I seeing?
2. Why does it matter to me?
3. What can I do with it now?
4. What must I not infer from it?

The product boundary remains: Eidome models and explains the person. Forge owns
exercises, programming and workout execution.

## Implemented first pass

- Added a dynamic “Why this matters” card to Body, Anatomy and Joints.
- Every mode now states a practical use, a limitation, and one next useful step.
- Anatomy guidance reacts to the currently selected structure.
- Body guidance reports remaining regional measurements.
- Joint guidance reports remaining mobility entries.
- Reframed “completeness” as input coverage so it cannot be mistaken for
  anatomical or medical confidence.
- Preserved the existing SceneKit interaction and mode-specific actions.

## Acceptance checks

- A first-time user can explain why each mode exists without opening Settings.
- No wording implies a scan, diagnosis, injury detection or measured anatomy.
- The next step changes with entered measurements, mobility data and anatomy
  selection.
- Body measurement entry and joint mobility entry remain one tap away.
- Dynamic Type and VoiceOver retain readable labels and logical order.

## Deferred

- Joint hit-testing and region-specific joint cards.
- Structure-to-movement maps backed by broader reviewed sources.
- Forge deep links for relevant exercises.
- Camera-state persistence beyond the current view lifetime.
