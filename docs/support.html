Eidome Support

Eidome builds a personal, estimated body model from information you choose to add.

Delete a twin

Open the profile selector, swipe left on a twin, choose Delete, and confirm.

Delete all data

Open About Eidome, choose Delete All Data, and confirm. You can also remove all local app data by deleting Eidome from your device.

Contact

Email support@eidome.com. Include your Eidome version, iOS version, device model, and a description of what happened. Do not email sensitive health information.
