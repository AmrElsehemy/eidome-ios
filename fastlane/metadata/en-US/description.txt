Explore 3D muscles, bones, and joints, and build an estimated body model from your own measurements.

Eidome brings reference anatomy and personal body measurements into one interactive workspace. Rotate and zoom anatomical layers, browse named structures, and record measurements and mobility information for each profile. Reference anatomy is educational; it is not a scan of your body.

CREATE YOUR TWIN
Start with a name, age, height, weight, and biological sex. Eidome generates an estimated body shape that gives you an immediate visual starting point.

EXPLORE IN LAYERS
Rotate and zoom your twin, then switch between Body, Muscles, Skeleton, and Joints. Anatomical layers are educational representations—not medical scans.

BUILD MULTIPLE PROFILES
Create separate twins for yourself and adult-managed dependents. Each profile keeps its own measurements, mobility information, and completion progress.

REFINE WHAT EIDOME KNOWS
Add body measurements and mobility assessments to improve your twin over time. Eidome distinguishes personal measurements from estimated anatomy so you can understand what the model actually knows.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
This version requires no account, advertising, analytics, or cloud sync. Profile information stays in local app storage on your device. Delete one twin—or all Eidome data—whenever you choose.

Eidome is designed for fitness education and personal tracking. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnoses or treatment recommendations.
