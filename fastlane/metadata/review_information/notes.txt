Eidome creates an estimated visual body model for fitness education and personal tracking. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment.

This version has no account, advertising, third-party analytics, or cloud sync. Profile information remains in local app storage on the device. The device owner may create adult-managed dependent profiles.

No sign-in or review credentials are required.

Suggested review flow:
1. Create a twin from the onboarding screen.
2. Rotate and zoom the 3D model.
3. Switch between Body, Muscles, Skeleton, and Joints.
4. Add body and mobility measurements.
5. Add a second profile and switch profiles.
6. Open About Eidome to review privacy information and delete all local data.
