Guideline 2.1 — Information Needed

Physical-device recording
A screen recording captured on a supported physical iPhone running the latest available iOS version is provided in the Resolution Center reply accompanying this submission. It begins with launching Eidome from the Home Screen and demonstrates the typical user flow and principal features.

Eidome has no account registration or login, public user-generated content, paid content, subscriptions, or in-app purchases.

Purpose, audience, and value
Eidome is a consumer fitness-education and personal body-tracking application. It helps adults interested in fitness and mobility organize body measurements and user-entered mobility observations and understand them through an interactive three-dimensional body and reference-anatomy experience.

Eidome is informational and educational. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, injury prediction, clinical measurement, or medical advice. The avatar is a parametric estimate rather than a body scan, and internal anatomy is reference material rather than a scan of the user.

Access and suggested review flow
No account, credentials, subscription, sample file, or internet connection is required.

1. Launch Eidome and create a local profile.
2. Open Body and review or add measurements that shape the estimated exterior model.
3. Rotate and zoom the 3D model, change the view, leave the screen or app, and return to see the safe view restored.
4. Open Anatomy, switch between Muscles and Skeleton, browse/search named structures, and select or isolate a structure.
5. Open Joints and review or enter user-measured mobility observations.
6. Create or switch to another local profile to verify that profile data and saved 3D views remain independent.
7. Use Reset view to restore the deterministic default camera.
8. Open About, Privacy, Support, and Asset Acknowledgements, then review the in-app deletion controls.

External services, tools, and platforms
Eidome does not depend on external authentication, payments, advertising, third-party analytics, cloud AI, remote data providers, or cloud sync for its core functionality. It uses Apple iOS frameworks and locally bundled 3D assets. Personal profile information and 3D workspace preferences are stored locally on the device.

Regional availability
Eidome provides the same functionality and content in every region where it is available. There are no region-specific accounts, features, subscriptions, or content restrictions.

Third-party material and authorization
